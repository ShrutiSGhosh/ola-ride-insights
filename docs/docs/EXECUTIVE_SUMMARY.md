🚖 Ola Ride Insights — Executive Summary
📊 Key Metrics

Total rides: ~103k (July dataset).

Successful rides: ~62k (62%).

Cancellations: ~29k (28%).

Driver cancellations: ~18k

Customer cancellations: ~10k

Driver not found: ~10k

Estimated revenue: ~₹35M (based on booking value).

Peak demand: Evenings (6–10 PM) and weekdays (Mon–Wed).

💳 Payments

Cash (~₹19M) dominates, followed by UPI (~₹14M).

Card usage is minimal (<2M).

Insight: Cash remains king, but UPI is quickly catching up → digital adoption trend.

🚗 Rides & Vehicles

Average ride distance: ~14–16 km for premium cars & bikes, ~7 km for autos.

Ride duration: ~255 mins on average (long-tail rides exist).

Outliers: Very high booking values (>₹1500) likely reflect premium rides, surge pricing, or anomalies.

⭐ Ratings

Driver Rating (avg): ~4.0

Customer Rating (avg): ~4.0

Ratings skew towards 5.0, suggesting either service quality is strong or ratings inflation exists.

❌ Cancellations — Key Drivers

By Vehicle Type: Higher cancellations in premium categories (Prime Sedan, Plus, SUV).

By Time: Commute hours (7–10 AM, 6–9 PM) see most cancellations.

By Payment: No significant differences; but Cash & UPI dominate absolute volume.

Takeaway: Driver-side issues during peak commute hours are the largest pain point.

👥 Customers

Top 10 customers spend up to ₹8,000/month.

Top 5% by Value: Fewer but higher-value trips.

Top 5% by Frequency: Shorter, regular rides (stable revenue stream).

Recommendation:

Offer premium perks to high-value riders.

Introduce loyalty programs for frequent riders.

📅 Time Insights

Weekly rides: ~23k/week, stable demand.

Day-of-week: Strongest Mon–Wed; weaker on weekends.

Opportunity: Boost weekend demand via targeted promotions.

⚠️ Key Recommendations

Reduce driver cancellations during peak hours with incentives & surge payouts.

Strengthen fleet maintenance to reduce incomplete rides (vehicle breakdowns).

Promote UPI adoption to accelerate shift away from cash.

Segment customers:

Reward frequent riders with loyalty points.

Provide premium service upgrades for high-value customers.

Review booking value anomalies (>₹1500) to confirm if valid or data errors.
